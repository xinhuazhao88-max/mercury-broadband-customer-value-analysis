# Mercury Broadband Customer Value Analysis

## Project Overview

This Business Analyst portfolio project analyses Mercury's broadband customer growth and customer value using quarterly operational data from Q1-25 to Q4-26.

The analysis focuses on the relationship between broadband customer growth, Broadband ARPU, product adoption and multi-product customer growth.

## Business Problem

Mercury has experienced strong growth in its broadband customer base. However, this growth has not translated into higher average revenue per broadband customer.

Broadband connections increased substantially while Broadband ARPU declined over the same period.

### Key Business Question

How can Mercury improve broadband customer value while maintaining sustainable customer growth?

## Project Objectives

- Analyse broadband customer growth.
- Analyse quarterly Broadband ARPU trends.
- Compare customer adoption across Mercury's products.
- Identify potential customer segment and cross-selling opportunities.
- Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Broadband connections increased by approximately 17.5%.
- Mobile connections increased by approximately 78.0%.
- Customers with two or more products increased by approximately 17.6%.
- Broadband ARPU declined by approximately 6.0%.
- Customer growth therefore did not directly translate into higher broadband customer value.

## Business Insights

The analysis suggests that Mercury has successfully expanded its customer base, but customer acquisition and customer monetisation are moving in different directions.

This creates an opportunity to improve customer value through customer segmentation, cross-selling and better monetisation strategies.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate the drivers of declining Broadband ARPU.
2. Strengthen cross-selling between broadband and other products.
3. Develop customer segments based on product adoption and customer value.
4. Monitor customer growth and ARPU together as complementary KPIs.
